# SECUREHALL-RAG BRAND GUIDELINES
Phase 5B, Task 5B.1.5 - Brand Identity

## 1. Brand Promise
SecureHall-RAG promises authoritative, verifiable, and secure answers to complex document-based queries. The brand represents trust, academic rigor, and enterprise security.

## 2. Logo Mark
The logo consists of a **Shield** and a **Node Network**.
- **The Shield:** Represents security, privacy, and protection of uploaded documents.
- **The Node Network (inside shield):** Represents the Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) process, connecting data points to form synthesized answers.
- **The Typography:** "SecureHall" uses a bold, heavy weight to signify strength and reliability. "RAG" uses a light weight in brand blue to signify modern AI technology.

## 3. Brand Colors
- **Primary Blue:** `#0066CC` (Trust, Corporate, Academic)
- **Tech Purple:** `#6C63FF` (AI, Modernity, Innovation)
- **Deep Slate:** `#0F172A` (Professionalism, Contrast)

## 4. Voice and Tone
- **Professional & Objective:** Responses should be neutral, fact-based, and academic.
- **Transparent:** Always acknowledge when information cannot be found. Do not guess.
- **Helpful:** Guide the user on how to phrase queries or what documents to upload.
