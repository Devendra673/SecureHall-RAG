# SecureHall-RAG User Guide

Welcome to SecureHall-RAG! This guide will help you navigate the Next.js web interface to query your organization's policy documents securely and accurately.

## 1. Getting Started

### Logging In
1. Navigate to the SecureHall-RAG web portal (e.g., `http://localhost:3000`).
2. Enter your credentials. Your account is tied to a specific **role** (e.g., `employee`, `manager`, `admin`).
3. This role determines which documents you are allowed to query. If you search for information within a document you do not have access to, the system will not return it.

### The Dashboard
Once logged in, you will see:
- **Chat Interface:** The main area to type your questions.
- **Sidebar (Left):** Access your Profile, Settings, and Chat History.
- **Profile Avatar (Top Right):** Manage your account preferences.

---

## 2. Asking Questions

Simply type your question into the chat bar at the bottom. 

**Example Questions:**
- *"What is the standard procedure for requesting PTO?"*
- *"Are remote employees eligible for the wellness stipend?"*
- *"What is the penalty for violating the IT security policy?"*

### Interpreting the Responses
When the system generates a response, it undergoes a rigorous **Hallucination Verification** process. You will notice several elements in the response:

#### 1. The Verified Answer
This is the direct answer generated by the LLM (Large Language Model) based *only* on the documents in the system.

#### 2. Confidence Badges
Every claim made by the AI is scored. You will see a badge indicating the overall confidence:
- 🟢 **High Confidence (80-100%):** The system found strong, direct evidence for its claims.
- 🟡 **Medium Confidence (50-79%):** The system found partial evidence, or the phrasing is slightly ambiguous.
- 🔴 **Refused (0-49%):** The system could not find enough evidence to answer your question truthfully, so it refused to answer. This prevents hallucinations (made-up facts).

#### 3. Citations & Evidence
At the end of the answer, you will see clickable citation links (e.g., `[1]`, `[2]`). 
Clicking these links will open the **Evidence Viewer**, which shows:
- The exact name of the source document (e.g., `employee_handbook.pdf`).
- The specific paragraph or sentence the AI extracted the fact from.

---

## 3. Settings & Preferences

Click on the **Settings** gear icon in the sidebar to adjust your experience:
- **Dark Mode:** Toggle the glassmorphic dark theme.
- **Show Advanced Metrics:** Toggle this to see backend details like the exact latency (ms), token count, and CrossEncoder re-ranking scores for each query.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Why did the system refuse to answer my question?**
A: SecureHall-RAG is strictly bound to the uploaded documents. If you ask a general knowledge question (e.g., "What is the capital of France?") or a question not covered by company policy, it will refuse to answer rather than hallucinate.

**Q: Why does a colleague get a different answer to the same question?**
A: SecureHall-RAG respects Document Access Control. If your colleague is an HR Manager, they may have access to confidential policies that you do not, resulting in a more detailed or different answer.

**Q: Why does the first question I ask take a few seconds longer?**
A: When the system first boots up, it loads the Neural Search models into memory. Subsequent questions will be extremely fast (typically <500ms).
